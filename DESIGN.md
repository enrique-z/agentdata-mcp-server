# Design System — AgentData Sales Dashboard

## Product Context
- **What this is:** A high-conversion B2B sales dashboard and landing page funnel for non-technical agencies.
- **Who it's for:** SDRs, Lead Gen Agencies, M&A Boutiques, and Go-To-Market teams.
- **Space/industry:** B2B Data / Lead Generation (Spain & EU).
- **Project type:** Web app / Marketing funnel.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Direction:** Industrial/Utilitarian
- **Decoration level:** Minimal (Typography and data do all the work)
- **Mood:** Serious, high-value, data-dense, and highly functional. It should feel like a "Bloomberg Terminal for B2B Leads."

## Typography
- **Display/Hero:** General Sans — Sharp, modern, aggressive.
- **Body:** Geist — Highly legible, technical feel.
- **UI/Labels:** Geist
- **Data/Tables:** JetBrains Mono — Perfect for aligning phone numbers and emails (tabular-nums).
- **Code:** JetBrains Mono
- **Loading:** Google Fonts / Fontshare API.

## Color
- **Approach:** Restrained / High Contrast
- **Primary:** `#00FF41` (Terminal Green) — Signals "raw data" and bypasses legacy corporate gatekeepers.
- **Secondary:** `#F5F5F5` (Surface Gray)
- **Neutrals:** Black `#000000` to White `#FFFFFF`
- **Dark mode:** Invert base colors. Terminal Green remains primary.

## Spacing
- **Base unit:** 4px
- **Density:** Compact
- **Scale:** 2xs(4) xs(8) sm(12) md(16) lg(24) xl(32) 2xl(48) 3xl(64)

## Layout
- **Approach:** Grid-disciplined
- **Grid:** 12 columns
- **Border radius:** sm:2px, md:4px, lg:8px

## Motion
- **Approach:** Minimal-functional
- **Easing:** ease-in-out
- **Duration:** micro(50-100ms)
